\noindent\textbf{The Problem}\quad
The public registry of physical attacks on Bitcoin holders lists \textbf{\attacksTotal} cases (\attacksAsOf), with a record \attacksRecordYearCount{} in \attacksRecordYear. \textbf{About 1 in 25 ends with someone dead}, and \attacksAbductNow\% of \attacksRecordYear's attacks involved abduction or captivity, up from \attacksAbductThen\% in 2021. When obscurity is the default, attackers \emph{eventually} learn that escalating violence pays (\href{\drurl}{The Deadly Race}; \href{\dsurl}{The Denial Spiral}).

\vfill

\noindent\textbf{Our Solution: Tacit Knowledge-Based Authentication (TKBA)}\\
Great Wall's TKBA protocol---free software under MIT/Apache-2.0---is the \textbf{first and only} system that simultaneously delivers:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Deviceless:} No hardware, seed phrase, or object to steal
    \item \textbf{Individual Custody:} True self-custody---no third-party reliance
    \item \textbf{Anti-Obscure:} Security \textit{increases} as attackers learn the protocol
    \item \textbf{Coercion-Resistant:} Requires time + tacit knowledge, neutralizing wrench attacks
\end{itemize}
The same mechanism, licensed to banks, protects customers who keep their money with an institution.
