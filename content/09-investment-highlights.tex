\section{Key Investment Highlights}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Solves a Deadly, Growing Problem:} The wrench attack is the visceral, unsolved problem of self-custody. The public registry lists \attacksTotal{} attacks, a record \attacksRecordYearCount{} in \attacksRecordYear, about 1 in 25 of them fatal\cite{lopp2024}.

    \item \textbf{Two Paths, Each With Its Own Proof:} The self-custody marketplace is first by readiness: a working beta, with demand native to crypto. Bank licensing is largest by size, and the regulator has already conceded the countermeasure: Brazil's central bank mandates delay against coerced transfers\cite{bcb2021res142}, so banks would be buying a better implementation of an existing obligation (Section~\ref{sec:market}).

    \item \textbf{One Protocol, Two Deployments, Three Revenue Streams:} A self-custody marketplace where users pay anonymously for convenience; custodial TKBA licensed to banks; and paid education, the entry point that proves demand from day one.

    \item \textbf{Building the Loud, Proud, and Free Movement:} Anti-obscurity turns the culture of fear around. Because the protocol works even when attackers know all about it, users can advertise it: our cards and apparel work like an alarm company's yard sign, and every visible user strengthens the deterrent for all.

    \item \textbf{Research-Backed Founder:} Author of Bitcoin \href{\bipurl}{BIP-450} and of \href{\drurl}{The Deadly Race} and \href{\dsurl}{The Denial Spiral}, starting a doctorate on the subject (Section~\ref{sec:founder}).

    \item \textbf{Capital-Efficient Marketplace Growth:} In the self-custody marketplace, Providers earn a \num{\providerCommissionPercent}\% lifetime commission on the clients they recruit, until we reach \numint{\referralProgramEndCustomers/1000}k paying customers. The model projects an LTV:CAC of \numint{\ltvCacRatio}:1 with a \num{\subPaybackMonths}-month payback.

    \item \textbf{Defensible Network Effects:} A growing base of reputable, anonymous Providers creates value that competitors cannot easily replicate.

    \item \textbf{A Staged Investment Opportunity:} A small pre-seed at \$\numint{\roundPreseedValuation/1000000}M funds the course, the Flying Turtle release and incorporation; the \$\numint{round(\seedAmount/1000,0)}K seed funds the custodial product and the first bank pilot; a Series A scales both, toward a \$\num{\targetValHigh}M valuation by Year 3.

    \item \textbf{Independent of the AI Cycle:} Value comes from verifiable protocol security and fee revenue, not from AI hype.

    \item \textbf{Strong Gross Margins:} The marketplace's \num{\subGrossMargin}\% gross margin, derived from explicit COGS components, sits within the 60--80\% marketplace benchmark range, with room to improve through Lightning Network payments.
\end{enumerate}
