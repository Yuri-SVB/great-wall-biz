\begin{frame}[t]{``Doesn't multisig solve this?''}
  \vspace{1.0cm}
  {\Large No --- it moves the problem.}\\[0.8cm]
  A co-signer who can be reached is a second victim, not a second lock.\\[0.4cm]
  A co-signer who \emph{cannot} be reached is a custodian, and you have
  given up self-custody to get there.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{``What about a duress PIN?''}
  \vspace{1.0cm}
  {\Large That is the failure mode, not the fix.}\\[0.8cm]
  A partial disclosure the attacker cannot verify gives them a reason to
  keep going. Graceful degradation --- a virtue everywhere else in
  engineering --- is, under coercion, an incentive to escalate.\\[0.5cm]
  \quiet{Blockstream ships a duress PIN. That is the strongest evidence I have
  that the problem is real.}
\end{frame}
