{\Large\textbf{The Visceral Problem: The Culture of Fear}}
\vspace{0.2cm}
{\color{accent}\hrule}
\vspace{0.22cm}

\noindent Self-custody today presents a false and dangerous choice:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Exchanges (Coinbase, Binance):} Convenience, but you give up your keys. ``Not your keys, not your coins.''
  \item \textbf{Hardware Wallets (Ledger, Trezor):} True self-custody, but you become a target. These are \emph{completely vulnerable to a wrench attack}.
\end{itemize}
This has created a culture of fear that forces sovereign individuals to hide in the shadows.

\vspace{0.28cm}

{\color{accent}\hrule}
\vspace{0.2cm}
{\Large\textbf{The Anti-Fragile Solution: Security Through Anti-Obscurity}}
\vspace{0.2cm}
{\color{accent}\hrule}
\vspace{0.22cm}

\noindent Our protocol is \textbf{TGPO --- the Time-Gated Perceptual Oracle}, and the property it delivers is \textbf{TKBA (Tacit Knowledge-Based Authentication)}: your mind is the only key. TGPO authenticates by \emph{recognition} rather than recall---you recognise something you cannot describe, so there is nothing to hand over under coercion---and a mandated delay means an attacker cannot convert a captive into an immediate transfer. The protocol is \emph{anti-fragile}: unlike obscurity-based security, its deterrence grows as attackers learn how it works. See \href{\faqurl}{our FAQ} for the mechanics.

\vspace{0.15cm}

\noindent\textbf{One mechanism, two deployments}, differing only in who counts failed attempts. \textbf{Custodial ($\sim$16 bits):} TGPO returns an authentication decision; the \emph{institution} imposes the delay, as it already rations PIN attempts. Four palettes of sixteen images, reshuffled each use, one pick per palette: $16^4 = 2^{16}$, against $10^4$ for a 4-digit PIN --- synthesised server-side, so it can use what humans recognise best, \textbf{faces and voices}. \textbf{Self-custodial ($\sim$160 bits):} TGPO returns a derived key you deploy yourself; nobody counts guesses against an encrypted seed, so the substrate must be high-entropy and derived deterministically on your own device. \emph{Status, plainly: a partial self-custodial prototype exists; the custodial deployment is designed but not built.}

\noindent We monetize TGPO through three segments, counted in three different units and never summed into one customer number:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
  \item \textbf{S1 --- Subscriptions} (individuals): the Anonymous Computation Marketplace. Clients pay a recurring subscription to outsource the self-custodial time-lock (2 to 168 hours); Providers monetize idle compute running those jobs. We take a \textbf{28\%} platform fee. \emph{The only segment in the Year 1--3 projections.}

  \item \textbf{S3 --- Capacity sales} (companies): solving capacity for \emph{digital} assets --- treasury keys, signing credentials, root secrets. Same take rate as S1, higher ARPU, lower count.
\end{itemize}
